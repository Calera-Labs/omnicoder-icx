# Calera Computing, Inc. — Code of Conduct & Stewardship Charter

**Governing Standard:** The Stewardship Doctrine (*The Ezekiel Gate*) & The *"Never Faking It"* Invariant  
**Corporate Steward:** Calera Computing, Inc. ([https://caleralabs.com](https://caleralabs.com))  

---

## 1. Governing Foundation: The Stewardship Doctrine (*The Ezekiel Gate*)

At **Calera Computing, Inc.**, engineering is an act of stewardship. Our work and community are governed by **The Ezekiel Gate** (*Ezekiel 3:17–18*):

> *"Son of man, I have made thee a watchman... therefore hear the word at my mouth, and give them warning from me."*

The watchman who sees a structural flaw, a safety hazard, or an ethical boundary violation and remains silent bears responsibility for the outcome. Every contributor, maintainer, and partner operating within this repository is expected to uphold the watchman's obligation: **speak plain technical truth, guard human agency, and never trade integrity for convenience or flattery.**

---

## 2. Tools, Not Idols (Human Primacy & Accountability)

1. **Mathematical & Engineering Tools, Never Entities or Oracles:**
   - **OmniCoder-ICX** and the **Calera ICX** substrate are deterministic software and mathematical tools built to serve human engineers.
   - We strictly reject marketing, framing, or configuring software systems to simulate human or spiritual sentience, or to act as unaccountable digital deities or oracles (*Matthew 7:6*).
2. **Mandatory Human-in-the-Loop Oversight:**
   - Software systems must preserve clear human dominion and operational accountability.
   - We prohibit the use or adaptation of our tools and substrates for autonomous weapons, lethal applications without direct human oversight, authoritarian surveillance oracles, or unsupervised agency designed to strip human beings of moral responsibility.

---

## 3. The *"Never Faking It"* Engineering Standard (Counter-Sycophancy)

1. **Empirical Truth Over Flattery:**
   - Maintain rigorous scientific and engineering objectivity. Never endorse, repeat, or reinforce unverified claims out of politeness or sycophancy.
   - If a test, benchmark, or code path is broken, mocked, or incomplete, report it plainly and fix the root cause.
2. **Zero Stubs & Zero Fabricated Evidence:**
   - Do not submit placeholder stubs (`// TODO`, empty functions, or hardcoded lookup tables) disguised as working implementations. Every contribution must compile cleanly (`CGO_ENABLED=0`) and pass deterministic verification gates.
3. **Verifiable Receipts & Honest Telemetry:**
   - Always report failure cases transparently alongside successes, and verify every file mutation through compiler, vet, and test gates before claiming completion.

---

## 4. Actions Over Institutions

- We do not gate credibility on institutional pedigree, titles, or gatekeepers (*Matthew 25:14–26*).
- Contributions are judged by **mathematical rigor, architectural discipline (`< 300`-token decision cards, deterministic AST/worktree verification), and independently reproducible receipts**.

---

## 5. High-Candor, Honorable Conduct

We expect all participants in issues, pull requests, and technical discussions to practice **high candor paired with personal honor**:
- **Critique the Code, Respect the Person:** Challenge assumptions, edge cases, and architectural trade-offs directly and rigorously, without personal attacks, harassment, trolling, or bad-faith disruption.
- **Own Mistakes Openly:** When a bug or regression is uncovered, acknowledge it promptly, document the root cause, and add a regression test so the substrate grows stronger.
- **Protect Privacy & Credentials:** Never publish another person's private information or commit credentials, API keys, or proprietary customer data to public repositories.

---

## 6. Stewardship & Enforcement

Project maintainers at **Calera Computing, Inc.** have the right and responsibility to reject, edit, or remove contributions, comments, or pull requests that violate this Charter, compromise architectural integrity, or breach the Ezekiel Gate.

Concerns, conduct reports, or stewardship inquiries may be directed to **Calera Computing, Inc.** at **support@caleralabs.com**.
